# tools/ — builder inputs (dev/agent notes)

Purpose: everything the Spanish pack build reads or writes, other than the
shared builder itself (`engine/tools/packbuilder`, `langs/es.py`). Read this
before touching any file here; it says what is hand-maintained vs generated.

## Files

- `build_pack.py` — shim. Runs `python3 -m packbuilder build --lang es --repo .`
  against `engine/tools` (or `$PACKBUILDER_PATH`). Hand-maintained, rarely changes.
- `requirements.txt` — hand-maintained. Shared builder deps plus the Spanish
  spaCy model (`es_core_news_sm`).
- `gloss_overrides.json` — hand-maintained. Fixes high-frequency gloss misses
  (ser "identity, nature, time" vs estar "state, location").
- `forced_a1.txt` — hand-maintained. A1 core word list forced into A1 (closed
  sets and fixed phrases live in `langs/es.py` instead).
- `passages_src.json` — hand-maintained. Source text for the 60 reading
  passages; `packbuilder passages` builds `pack/passages.json` from it.
- `id_map_v1.json` — generated, then frozen 2026-09-24 from the first
  shipped build. Maps (lemma, POS) to a fixed word id across rebuilds. Never
  hand-edit; never renumber.
- `REPORT.md` — generated on every full build by `build_pack.py`. Everything
  outside the `manual` markers is regenerated; the manual section (QA
  verdicts, check output) is preserved across runs.
- `REPORT_passages.md` — generated by `packbuilder passages`. Per-passage
  coverage numbers and QA notes.

## Rebuild

See CLAUDE.md's "Commands" section for the pinned invocations. Sources
download once into `.cache/` (gitignored) and are reused; the build is
deterministic (two runs with different `PYTHONHASHSEED` give byte-identical
`pack/*.json`). `build_pack.py --stage {corpus,tag,lex,freq,words,all}`
stops after one stage, using cached intermediate output under `.cache/derived/`.
The first spaCy pass over the 261k translated sentences takes about 6
minutes and is cached; later runs take 1-2 minutes.

## How words, senses and links are chosen

1. Tatoeba Spanish sentences with an English translation are tagged with
   spaCy `es_core_news_sm`. Lemmas are checked against Wiktionary inflection
   data. Clitic compounds resolve to the verb (dímelo, hacerlo, dándole).
   Where two lemmas share a form (creo: creer/crear), corpus lemma votes
   break the tie. Pronoun surfaces the tagger mangles (vosotros tagged
   NOUN, contigo tagged PROPN) are fixed by a closed table. A bare
   clause-initial noun with a verb reading is the verb ("Estudio inglés").
2. Each frequency-list surface is split over the (lemma, POS) pairs it takes
   in context, including accent-split pairs (si/sí, el/él, solo/sólo). Only
   cardinals count as numerals: ambos and medio keep their own POS.
3. The Wiktionary entry matching the corpus POS gives the gloss. Senses are
   ranked by overlap with the English translations of sentences using the
   word. A sense tagged for Spain, Latin America, or three or more countries
   is standard (ordenador, computadora); narrower regional senses rank last.
   `gloss_overrides.json` fixes high-frequency misses.
4. A lemma gets a second entry when another POS holds at least 20% of its
   tokens with a distinct sense. When a sentence could link either entry
   (solo "only" vs "alone"), the English translation picks the one whose
   sense it names. Verbs used with se in at least 60% of linked sentences
   show as -se (enterarse); others revert to the base.
5. Nouns carry their article: el/la, el agua "(f)" for stressed a-, el/la
   for common gender (el/la estudiante), los/las for pluralia tantum. A noun
   whose two genders are different words (el frente, la frente) links only
   when the sentence shows its gender.
6. Fixed phrases (sin embargo, por favor, a pesar de, muchas gracias, en
   absoluto...) are taught as phrases. Matching is by token after splitting
   del/al. Nouns bound to a phrase (embargo, supuesto) are not taught alone.
7. Closed sets are forced into A1: days, months, seasons, numbers 0-20 +
   tens + cien/ciento/mil, colours, nationalities, greetings, the full
   personal pronoun paradigm (vosotros/vosotras, nosotras, ellas,
   conmigo/contigo), possessives, demonstratives, plus `forced_a1.txt`.
   Everything else is levelled by blended frequency rank: A1 600, A2 700, B1 700.
8. Sentences are 4-14 tokens (5+ at B1). Pretérito is kept. Voseo and
   regional slang are kept out of A1/A2. Profanity, non-standard English
   register, and translations whose pronoun gender contradicts the Spanish
   are dropped.

The generic algorithm (level bands, stages, how to add a language) is in
`engine/tools/packbuilder/README.md`.

## Reading passages

`packbuilder passages .` builds `pack/passages.json` from `passages_src.json`
and writes `REPORT_passages.md`. It links word ids the same way as Tatoeba
sentences, and enforces in-pack coverage of at least 95% at A1/A2 and 93% at
B1, plus a level budget (an A1 passage may use at most 3 A2 words, an A2
passage at most 3 B1 words). The Spanish-specific passage linker rules
(phrase-part links, surface-reading fallback, truecase after «¡¿ and !?,
passage-only fue/fuera resolution) live in `langs/es.py`'s passage hooks, not
in this repo. Regenerate `pack/*.js` afterward (jsonify_pack.py appends
passages into sentences.js).

## Known deviations from the ideal spec

- The es spaCy model is GPL-3.0. It runs only at build time; nothing from it
  ships except derived pack data.
- Almost no permissively licensed Spanish Tatoeba audio exists (most is CC
  BY-NC-ND or unlicensed), so the trainer relies on browser TTS/STT (es-ES).
- Same-spelling pairs (solo adv/adj, sí intj/pron, bajo prep/adj) make the
  engine validator print "share surface form" warnings; that is expected.
- Fixed phrases rank after the frequency words (rank is informational).
- `revert_dedupe_gloss` is on for Spanish only (see `../TODO.md` "Builder").
